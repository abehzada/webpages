Zoom Netiquette


 

Please consider the following guidelines and best practices to ensure a supportive and effective learning environment:

  • Please arrive 5 minutes early to Zoom sessions to log in and test your audio and video, so sessions can begin on time.
  • Keep your microphone muted unless speaking to reduce background noise.
  • If possible, turn your camera on. It helps me (your instructor) judge how I am doing with engagement. If you are not comfortable with your "space" showing in the background, consider turning on a virtual background image in Zoom.
  • Take care that your zoom camera does not display inappropriate images that may be regarded by others as distracting, inflammatory, or threatening to the safety of the learning environment.
