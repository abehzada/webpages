Guidelines for Requesting a Letter of Recommendation


Dear Students,

Each year I receive a large number of requests for letters of recommendation. I take this responsibility very seriously, as a strong letter can have a meaningful impact on your future opportunities. To ensure that the process is efficient for both you and me—and to avoid any conflicts of interest—I have established the following policies:

1. Advance Notice

If you would like me to write a letter of recommendation, you must notify me at least one month before the earliest deadline. Requests made with less than one month’s notice cannot be accommodated.

2. Academic Requirement

You must have taken at least one course with me and earned a letter grade of A. Otherwise, I will not be able to write you a strong letter.

3. Attendance Requirement

If you missed more than two lectures in any course you took with me, I will not be able to write a strong letter for you. Your attendance—and your attitude toward attendance—will be reflected in any recommendation I write.

4. Required Materials

At least one month before the deadline, please email me the following:

  • CV or résumé
  • Transcript (unofficial is acceptable)
  • List of all courses taken with me, including the semester you took each course and your letter grade
  • List of all programs, institutions, or positions you are applying to, including:
    • type of position (e.g., graduate program, scholarship, job)
    • deadlines for each recommendation
    • any specific submission instructions

5. Current Enrollment Policy

If you are currently enrolled in a course with me, I am not able to submit a recommendation letter for you at this time. Doing so could create a potential conflict of interest with the course evaluations you submit at the end of the semester. In addition, I must wait until the semester is completed in order to form a fair and complete assessment of your performance.

Thank you!
